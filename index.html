ABOUT US

Our Story

Three Generations of Flour on Our Hands.

The Krum Kartel began as a weekend market stall in 2019 and grew into a full Sandton storefront by popular (and persistent) demand. Every recipe on our shelves has been tested, argued over, and eventually approved by the whole crew before it earns a permanent spot in the vault.
