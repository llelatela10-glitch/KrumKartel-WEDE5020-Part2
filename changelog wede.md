# Current website Analysis

There isn't a website for The Krum Kartel Bakery at the moment. The company only uses phone pre-orders, in-store walk-ins, and an unofficial Instagram account (@TheKrumKartel_012), which has about 1,200 followers. The Instagram account is not a suitable replacement for a company website, even though it does have some visuals that can provide some marketing value.

According to (Networksolutions 2026) study indicates, 39% of firms will invest in web development in 2026, and this is due to the increasing importance of online customer experience.

Therefore, The Krum Kartel should realize that it will probably lose a digital advantage to competitors if it delays.

# Proposed features and functionality

• Homepage — hero image of signature products, brief brand story, featured items, and a "Pre-Order Now" button.

• Menu/Products — categorised product gallery (breads, pastries, cakes) with descriptions and pricing.

• Custom Cakes — dedicated page with a cake enquiry/quote request form.

• About Us — founder story, sourcing philosophy, and team photographs.

• Contact & Find Us — store address, opening hours, embedded Google Maps, phone number, and email.

• Blog/Recipes — optional page for seasonal recipes and baking tips

According to (Convergine, 2025), As mobile devices are expected to account for more than 62% of all internet traffic worldwide by 2025 .

According to (Zippia, 2026) Studies show that nearly 40% of visitors are lost before the page renders if a site takes too long to load.

This website will be built for mobile first and fast loading times .

# Technical requirements

<www.TheKrumKartel.co.za> is the domain name.

Hosting: R80–R150 per month, including free SSL, for shared hosting with Xneelo or Vox Telecom.

Languages & Frameworks: JavaScript for the product image gallery, sticky header, and pre-order form validation; HTML5 for page structure; CSS3 for layout and responsive design.

SEO were removed from the technical requirement because the cost was too high to be implemented.
